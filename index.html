How to Play

  • Set Up: Arrange the board as shown below:
  • Join or Create a Room: Players can either join an existing room or create a new one. The round will start once at least 3 players are present.
  • Round Start: When the round begins, one player will be randomly selected as the Assassin.
  • Role Indicators:All non-Assassin players will see a word that is the round’s secret word, as shown below:
  • First Player: The first player to start the round is either the Assassin from the previous round or the player who most recently watched a spy movie.
  • Guessing the Word: The first player will say one word that hints at the round’s secret word. The Assassin will also say a word to blend in without revealing the secret word. The word cannot be:
    • The secret word itself.
    • A word that has already been said.
    • More than one word.
  • Next Turn: Moving clockwise, each player will say one word in turn.
  • Debate Time: After everyone has spoken, the players will begin debating who they believe the Assassin is. This is your chance to play detective and challenge each other’s arguments.
  • Vote: After a few minutes of debating, each player votes by pointing to the person they think is the Assassin.
  • Reveal: The player with the most votes must reveal their phone screen. If the Assassin is correctly identified, they are captured. However, if the Assassin manages to escape, the game continues. The Assassin will then have one final chance to guess the secret word. If they guess correctly, they escape.
  • Tiebreaker: In the event of a tie vote, the first player has the deciding vote.
