Assassin

How to play

Step 1 of 4

Set up the table

Lay out 25 Codenames word cards in a 5×5 grid. Put red agent cards in the top corners and blue ones in the bottom corners, so everyone can line their phone up with the table.

One player creates a room and shares its name, and everyone else joins it. You need 3 to 8 players.

One of you is the Assassin

When the host starts a round, everyone checks their phone. Agents find the highlighted card on the table. In this example the secret word is SEAL. The Assassin doesn't know it and has to bluff.

  • A"Ocean"
  • B"Stamp"
  • C"Animal"A bit vague…
  • D"Navy"

Give a clue, then vote

Going clockwise, everyone says one word linked to the secret word. Don't say the word itself or repeat a clue. Last round's Assassin goes first. In the first round, it's whoever most recently watched a spy movie.

Then debate. On the count of three, everyone points at their suspect. If there's a tie, the tied players each give one more clue, then everyone votes again between them. If it's still tied, the first player decides.

  • +2 Wrong player accused. The Assassin escapes and scores 2.
  • +1 Caught, but guesses the word. The Assassin gets one guess. If it's right, they still escape and score 1.
  • +1 Caught, wrong guess. Every agent scores 1.

Reveal and score

The accused player shows their phone. The first player to 5 points wins. For the next round, flip all 25 cards to use the words on the back. After both sides have been played, deal 25 new cards.
